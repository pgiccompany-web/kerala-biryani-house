About Us

Our Story

Kerala Biryani House brings the traditional flavours of Malabar to Vadakara. Every pot is prepared in the dum style, sealed and cooked slowly so the rice absorbs every layer of spice.

We use fresh ingredients, hand-ground masalas, and recipes passed down through generations of Malabar kitchens.
